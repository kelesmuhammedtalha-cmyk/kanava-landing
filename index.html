The line

Five stages, four stops

It is a production line, not an autopilot. Nothing moves to the next stage without you.

  1. 01

    Topic

    Ten candidates in your niche — title, angle, and why it should land — checked against everything your channel already published. A topic you reject never comes back, and the reason feeds the next round.

    You pick one
  2. 02

    Script

    Scene by scene — hook, body, CTA — in your channel's tone and length format. Edit any scene inline, regenerate a single scene without touching the rest.

    You approve the script
  3. 03

    Visuals + voice

    A prompt and an image per scene, held to your style set; your voice reads the script with per-scene timing and a pronunciation dictionary. Regenerate one scene, regenerate the batch, or drop in your own image.

    You sign off scene by scene
  4. 04

    Render

    16:9 or 9:16, word-level subtitles with optional karaoke burn-in. The queue shows progress, and a failed render resumes from the scene it died on instead of starting over.

  5. 05

    Publish kit

    Three title variants, description, tags, three thumbnails, a pinned-comment suggestion and a publish-time suggestion. Download the mp4, the SRT, the images and the text.

    You ship it
